Sandals, Clogs & Slip-Ons

Flat flip-flops give the arch no support and make the toes grip to keep them on, a common trigger for heel pain and tendon strain. Look for a contoured footbed, a deep heel cup and a back strap. Closed-toe styles are safer for people with diabetes or neuropathy.
