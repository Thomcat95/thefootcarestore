05 · Circulation support

Compression Socks

Good for swelling, long travel days, and standing jobs. The strongest compression isn't always the best choice — I've found many patients stop wearing 20–30 mmHg socks entirely because they're too hard to put on and take off, even when prescribed. A lower, more tolerable compression that actually gets worn daily often beats a stronger one sitting in a drawer.
