Standing & Posture

Prolonged standing is one of the most common drivers of plantar fasciitis and heel pain I see in clinic — and prolonged sitting with poor posture feeds into the same lower-body chain (hips, knees, ankles). These are the everyday changes I recommend alongside footwear, not instead of it.

Standing desk

Anti-Fatigue Standing Mat

A contoured mat (like the well-known Topo by Ergodriven) that encourages small shifts in position through the day — much easier on the feet than a flat mat or bare floor.
