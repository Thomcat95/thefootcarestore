Sports Shoes

Each sport loads the foot differently. A running shoe is built for straight-ahead motion and can roll an ankle on a pickleball or tennis court. Using the right shoe for the sport is one of the simplest ways to prevent sprains, blisters and overuse injuries.

Pickleball

Pickleball is quick side-to-side movement, and running shoes roll the ankle on lateral cuts. Wear a court shoe with a low, wide base, a reinforced toe, and a non-marking outsole.
