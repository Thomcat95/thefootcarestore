Seasonal · November to February

Winter boots

For cold and wet weather, look for a waterproof upper, a deep-lug rubber sole for grip on ice, and enough room for a thicker sock without squeezing the toes. Tight, cold boots reduce circulation, which matters most for people with diabetes or poor circulation.
