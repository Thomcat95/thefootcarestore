Product of the Month · October 2026
Tactical & work boots

Garmont T8 Bifida

A lightweight tactical boot built on mountain-boot construction, widely recommended by service members for long ruck marches and field training.

Why it's this month's pick: Hunting season, cooler weather and long days on rough ground make October a good time to look at boot support. The T8 Bifida gives ankle support without the weight of a traditional combat boot, and it meets AR 670-1, the Army's rule for optional combat boots, in coyote brown.

Good fit for: Military and law enforcement personnel, hunters, hikers carrying heavy packs, and anyone who wants ankle support in a lighter boot. Regular and wide widths are available.

Skip it if: You need a waterproof boot for cold, wet conditions, or a steel or composite safety toe for work. Look at an insulated or safety-toe boot instead.

Key details:

  • 8-inch shaft, 1.8 mm suede leather with polyester panels
  • Vibram rubber outsole and OrthoLite Ultra footbed
  • About 1.5 lb per boot (size 9); built to drain and dry quickly rather than to be waterproof
  • US sizes 4 to 15 in half sizes, regular and wide; a Marine Corps EGA version is also made

What to look for in any tactical boot:

  • An 8-inch shaft and coyote color if it's for Army uniform wear
  • A firm heel counter and a shank for support under load
  • Room for the socks you'll actually wear, and for an orthotic if you use one
  • A break-in period of a week or two before long marches

Compared with: Rocky S2V (made in USA and Berry compliant, heavier), Danner Tachyon (lighter, quick to break in), and Salomon Quest 4D Forces (more of a hiking boot, with extra cushioning).
