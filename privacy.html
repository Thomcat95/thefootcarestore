This Privacy Policy explains how Kurios Medical Supplies LLC ("we," "us," "our"), the operator of thefootcarestore.com and "The Foot Care Store" (the "Site"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit the Site. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, the Site may automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and general usage data (such as pages viewed and time spent on the Site), typically through standard hosting logs and analytics tools.

Information you provide. The Site includes a "Suggest a Product" form where you may voluntarily submit a product suggestion along with your name and email address (both optional). We use this solely to review suggestions and, if you provide an email address, to follow up with you if needed.

The Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to support basic site functionality, analytics, and affiliate link tracking (for example, to accurately credit purchases made through our affiliate partners, including Amazon and other retail partners). You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not function properly without them.

We use collected information to:

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through the Site, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of any information you provide.

7. Children's privacy

The federal law COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) regulates the collection of personal information from children under 13. In keeping with COPPA, the Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Your choices

You may adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being used. If the Site offers an email signup, you may opt out of future communications at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in those emails.
